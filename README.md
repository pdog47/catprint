# catprint

a one-page web app for printing photos and text on an MXW01 bluetooth "cat printer" (48mm, 384px) straight from your phone. no install, no server, nothing leaves your device.

open it: https://pdog47.github.io/catprint/

## where it works

- chrome on android, and chrome or edge on desktop
- iphone: safari can't do web bluetooth, so open the link in the free [bluefy](https://apps.apple.com/app/bluefy-web-ble-browser/id1492822055) browser

## what it does

- photos in three modes: line art (sharp threshold), photo (dots), comic (ink lines over dots)
- typed text with size and alignment
- darkness slider, battery level, paper and overheat warnings
